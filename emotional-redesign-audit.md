# Chelonaki emotional redesign audit

## Reference character

- Warm limestone stage with a deep navy chapter beneath it
- Editorial headline on the left, architectural depth on the right
- Ionic column, arch and turtle medallion read as one constructed world
- Thin physical folio leaves carry the strategic story
- The curved stone threshold makes the transition into the service chapter feel authored
- Navigation is quiet, precise and secondary to the brand world

## Current implementation

### Preserve

- Chelonaki wordmark, turtle symbol and Cormorant/Manrope pairing
- Ivory, navy, bronze and warm stone palette
- Existing information architecture, anchors, contact form and legal dialogs
- Strong column asset and the already approved wall medallion
- Founder-led positioning and the four connected fields

### Retire or reduce

- The hero ledger, which reads like a generic service directory and occupies the emotional focal area
- Repeated identical fade-up reveals across almost every section
- The isolated freestanding column without the arch, medallion and material context from the reference
- Generic agency phrases that could belong to many studios
- Repeated rectangular section boundaries where the reference uses a sculpted threshold

## Direction

- Design variance: 8
- Motion intensity: 7
- Information density: 3
- Type character: editorial, architectural, restrained
- One authored sticky sequence: the four capabilities move through a fixed architectural stage
- Hero motion: headline mask, architectural reveal, three meaningful folio leaves and a subtle medallion light shift
- Personal voice: first-person founder passages connecting AI, design and nutritional science

## Responsive intent

- Desktop: full architectural hero and sticky capability theatre
- Tablet: simplified architectural composition with medallion and folios retained
- Mobile: linear narrative, no sticky dependency, shorter transitions and clear tap targets
- Reduced motion: all content visible, no parallax or staged transforms

## SEO and behavior baseline

- Preserve the current single H1 and section anchor structure
- Preserve working navigation, mobile dialog, contact validation and legal dialogs
- Do not add tracking, external embeds or cookie-requiring services
