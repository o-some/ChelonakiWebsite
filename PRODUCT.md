# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Chelonaki serves organizations and brands that want to apply artificial intelligence to concrete business, product, communication or brand work. The exact primary launch segment within this group is still an open decision.

## Product Purpose

Chelonaki is a founder-led company that selects, develops and applies useful AI. It combines AI applications and consulting with content, marketing, web design and brand development. A later business phase may add nutrition and food-related ventures.

Success means that visitors quickly understand the breadth as one coherent offer, trust the founder's judgment and initiate a qualified conversation.

## Positioning

Chelonaki connects AI strategy, implementation, brand work and content under one founder-led system. Nutrition-related work is differentiated by the founder's training as a nutrition scientist rather than by a generic wellness claim.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients evaluate Chelonaki through the website, review its fields of work and contact the founder about a potential project. The current site is a marketing and inquiry surface, not an e-commerce shop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities:

- AI strategy and consulting
- custom applications, prototypes and automation
- AI-supported social media content
- AI-supported marketing and campaign systems
- web design, positioning and brand development
- nutrition concepts and later ventures

Constraints and open decisions:

- The current launch must not claim existing customers, results, certifications, prices or case-study metrics that have not been supplied.
- Food distribution is a later phase and requires a separate commerce and regulatory launch gate.
- The production hosting provider, contact-form delivery, retention periods and any optional analytics or embedded services are not yet confirmed.
- The exact legal entity, provider details and launch audience must be supplied before publication.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Chelonaki.
- Preserve the existing turtle/column logo assets.
- Binding visual references: Greek style, old-money restraint, logo-derived Aegean blue, gold and ivory. Dark surfaces must remain within the Aegean ramp rather than introducing a separate navy palette.
- The user explicitly prefers a light opening composition and a strong architectural column.
- The work should feel premium and distinctive while showing as few recognisable AI-design clichés as possible.
- The brand may use the phrase “The future needs roots.”

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing website implementation in `src/App.jsx` and `src/styles.css`
- Existing local logo assets in `public/assets/`
- Existing generated architectural and venture imagery in `public/assets/`
- Current-site screenshots in `qa/redesign-audit/`
- Three approved-for-selection redesign previews generated in the current design workflow
- No supplied customer logos, testimonials, commercial metrics, formal case studies or certifications

## Product Principles

1. Human judgment leads; technology serves the problem.
2. Broad capabilities must read as one disciplined system, not a list of unrelated services.
3. The work must demonstrate useful specificity without inventing proof.
4. Nutrition-related work must stay scientifically credible and avoid medical overclaiming.
5. Privacy, accessibility and legal readiness are part of the product rather than footer decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website should target WCAG 2.2 AA, support keyboard and touch interaction, preserve clear focus states and provide a full reduced-motion experience. The exact application of the German BFSG depends on the later consumer offer and company size; the build should nevertheless avoid accessibility debt from the start.
